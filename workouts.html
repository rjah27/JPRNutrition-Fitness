One of the most important things to establish when looking for a systematic workout plan for yourself, is finding a good balance of variation, movements, and stimulus in a workout.

Here I will present some a split that hits a lot of those key points one can use and enjoy while working out certain muscle groups.

"Push Workouts" or workouts that primarily target The Pectoral, The Triceps, and The Shoulder/Deltoid Muscles:

Smith Machine Incline Press

Machine Flat Chest Press

Low-High Cable Flies

Cable Tricep Push Down

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Overhead Cable Tricep Extension

*Workouts that target a larger area of "push" muscles such as the Smith Machine Incline, are considered Compound Movements. Other workouts as such: Barbell Bench Press, Barbell Incline Press, Barbell Shoulder Press. Compound Lifts do not have to be used as a daily variation since they can be more fatiguing, but having 1-2 compound variations per week is good for training. Each lift does not have to be incorporated into a single "Push" day, but can be varied throuhgout during the week.

"Pull Workouts" or workouts that primarily target The Back/Posterior, The Biceps, and The Lateral Deltoid Muscles:

Pull Ups

Seated Machine Rows

Seated Cable Rows

Plated Bar, Preacher Curls

Dumbbell Bicep Curls

Cable Lateral Raise

*Workouts that target a larger area of "pull" muscles such as Barbell Rows or Pull Ups, are considered Compound Movements. Other workouts as such: Plated Machine Rows, Bent over Rows, Deadlifts, Lat Pull Downs. Compound Lifts do not have to be used as a daily variation since they can be more fatiguing, but having 1-2 compound variations per week is good for training. Each lift does not have to be incorporated into a single "Pull" day, but can be varied throuhgout during the week.

"Lower Workouts" or workouts that primarily target the Quadricep, Hamstring, Adductor/Abductor, and Calf Muscles:

Barbell Back Squat

Hack Squat

Leg Extension

Seated Leg Curls

Barbell Romanian Deadlift

Standing Calf Raise

*Workouts that target a larger area of "lower" or leg muscles such as Barbell Squats, and Hack Squats, are considered Compound Movements. Other workouts as such: Leg Press Machine, Romanian Deadlifts, Pendulum Squat Machine, Bulgarian Split Squats. Compound Lifts do not have to be used as a daily variation since they can be more fatiguing, but having 1-2 compound variations per week is good for training. Each lift does not have to be incorporated into a single "Lower" day, but can be varied throuhgout during the week.
